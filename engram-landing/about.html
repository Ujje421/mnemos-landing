The Vision

Artificial Intelligence has reached an inflection point in reasoning capability. Modern LLMs can write complex algorithms, debug obscure errors, and architect entire systems. However, they suffer from a fundamental flaw: Amnesia.

Every time a developer opens a new chat session with an AI assistant, they have to start from zero. They have to re-explain their tech stack, their database schema, their naming conventions, and their specific architectural decisions. This repetitive context-loading creates massive friction in the development cycle.

At Engram Technologies, our vision is to solve the state problem. We are building the infrastructure that allows AI to remember everything about your codebase, permanently.

Mnemos: Our Flagship Product

Mnemos is the realization of our vision. It is a lightweight, local-first application that sits quietly on your machine, indexing your codebase, configurations, and git history into a persistent, structured knowledge graph.

By leveraging the Model Context Protocol (MCP), Mnemos acts as a universal memory server. Whether you use Claude Desktop, Cursor, or any other modern AI tool, Mnemos ensures that your AI assistant has instant, accurate context without you ever having to type a prompt.

The Team

We are a small, dedicated group of software engineers and AI researchers who felt the pain of "AI amnesia" every single day. We were tired of copy-pasting the same context into chat windows. We built Mnemos because it was the tool we desperately needed for our own workflows.

We are committed to building local-first software. Your code is your intellectual property, and we believe it shouldn't have to live on our servers for you to get the benefits of AI memory.
