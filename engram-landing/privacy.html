Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Your Code Stays Yours

Mnemos is a local-first application. Your codebase, architectural decisions, and AI conversations are stored locally on your machine in a local database (SQLite). We do not transmit your code to our servers. Your project's context never leaves your device unless you explicitly send it to an external AI provider.

2. Telemetry and Usage

The desktop application may collect anonymous, aggregated crash reports to help us improve stability. You can opt out of this entirely in the application settings. We do not track your specific queries or code.

3. Waitlist Data

If you sign up for our early access waitlist, we only collect your email address. We will only use this to notify you about product updates and your access status. We will never sell your email to third parties or spam you.

4. Third-Party AI Providers

Mnemos connects to external LLMs (like OpenAI or Anthropic) via API keys you provide, or locally via tools like Ollama. When you use external providers, your prompts and injected context are subject to their respective privacy policies. Mnemos itself acts as a local proxy and does not intercept or read this data.
